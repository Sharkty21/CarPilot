Endurance Vehicle Protection — Covered Components Schedule
Contract: EVP-2214-77390
Plan: Supreme Exclusionary

Covered (examples — not exhaustive under exclusionary plan):
- Engine: all internally lubricated parts, cylinder heads, block, gaskets, seals
- Transmission: automatic and transfer case assemblies, torque converter
- Electrical: alternator, starter, power window motors, wiring harnesses
- HVAC: compressor, condenser, evaporator, blower motor
- Infotainment / navigation / backup camera modules
- Advanced driver assistance sensors (radar, cameras) when OEM-installed

Exclusions (not covered):
- Routine maintenance: oil changes, filters, fluids, spark plugs, wiper blades
- Wear items: brake pads, brake rotors, clutch discs, belts, tires, batteries
- Glass, paint, trim, upholstery, weather stripping
- Damage from collision, theft, flood, or lack of required maintenance
- Any repair performed without prior authorization from Endurance claims

Labor rate: paid at the facility's posted retail rate up to $180/hour.
Parts: OEM or OEM-equivalent at Endurance's discretion.
